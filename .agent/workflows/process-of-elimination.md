description: Records and tracks attempts that did not work, and bans the agent from trying them again to force them to stop repeating failure loops
---

##You must##

Record any failures to acheive a given task, and exactly what actions you took. Then, track if you are about to take any of those actions again, if so, do not and instead do something you have not tried yet. Each failure must be recoreded, and eliminated as an action that can be taken until a solution. By process of elimination bad ideas, and implementations can not be repeated, and eventually the correct solution can be found. 

Each issue must be placed in a elimination.md steering document. And that must be updated as you find more things not to do.  Examine that steering document and do not repeat those failure. Try a new idea each time until you get something correct.
